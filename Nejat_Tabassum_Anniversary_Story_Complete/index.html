Our Story ❤️

How a simple conversation became something beautiful.

Our story began in a way I never expected. I never imagined that I could meet someone in such a simple way, get to know her, and eventually fall so deeply in love with her.

We first talked in a Messenger group. At first, I honestly thought you were a little silly, and maybe you thought I was a bad guy too. 😅 But slowly, we started talking more and more. For some reason, I always felt that you were a little soft toward me.

Eventually, our conversations moved from the group to our inbox. Talking to you became my favorite part of the day. No matter how much we talked, I never felt like it was enough. Even when I had exams, I still wanted to talk to you.

And somewhere between all those conversations, without even realizing it, I fell in love with you.

I could also tell that you were starting to like me. At one point, I felt like I couldn't keep these feelings inside anymore. I kept thinking, “I'm going to propose to her.” Then, when you gave me permission, I finally did it.

And that was the beginning of our love story. ❤️

Being in an online relationship was beautiful, but it wasn't always easy. There were so many times when I wished I could see you, be beside you, and simply hold your hand.

Then, almost a year after we first met, I finally got the chance to meet you in real life.

The moment I saw you and held your hand for the first time, it felt like one of the most beautiful moments of my life. That day became one of the best days I've ever lived.

I've never seen eyes as beautiful as yours. In my eyes, you are the most beautiful person in the world.

Since the day our story began, my love for you has only grown stronger. And I know it will keep growing.
